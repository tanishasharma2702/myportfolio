About Me

A curious first-year engineer blending analytical logic with creative vision.

I am a first-year engineering student at JECRC University, pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering with specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (Samatrix).

My deep curiosity about how machines learn, reason, and solve complex real-world challenges is what drove me to choose this specialization from day one. I believe in establishing deep, grounded fundamentals before moving ahead.

I have developed a strong command of C and C++, which gives me a rigorous understanding of structured programming, memory efficiency, and algorithmic thinking. Currently, I am actively learning Python to step into the ecosystem of data and modern AI libraries.

Beyond the terminal, creativity has always been a central part of my life. I have been drawing and painting for years. Far from being separate worlds, art has sharpened my patience, attention to detail, and ability to think out of the box—qualities that directly influence how I approach coding and critical problem solving. My aspiration is to combine technology, AI, and creativity as I learn and grow.
